# INSIGHT Risk – Customer Question List

## Core Questions

### First impression

- What is your first impression of this result?
- What do you think this result is trying to communicate?
- Is the meaning clear at first glance?

### Traditional risk assessment

- Do you currently use traditional risk assessment?
- If yes, which model or models do you use?
- How do you use them in workflow today?

### Expectations for image-based risk prediction

- What are your expectations for an image-based risk prediction model?
- What should it improve compared with traditional models?

### Cutoff perception

- What feels like an appropriate high-risk cutoff for image-based risk prediction?
- Is `1.7%`, `3.0%`, or another value more appropriate?
- Should one threshold be shown, or more than one?

### Workflow fit

- In which platform should this result be shown?
- Would it fit best in PACS, RIS, EMR, or somewhere else?
- Why?

### Prototype preference

- Which prototype is the most intuitive?
- Why?
- Which prototype would fit best in your workflow?

### Safety and interpretation

- Does any version feel likely to be over-interpreted?
- Does any version feel too simplified?
- Does the wording feel appropriately neutral?

### Closing

- If you could change one thing, what would it be?
- What additional information would you want before using this in practice?

## Prototype-Specific Questions

## Prototype 1

- Is estimated risk alone enough?
- Would you want any additional context?

## Prototype 2

- Is a fixed `3.0%` cutoff easier to use?
- Does a fixed binary label feel too limiting?

## Prototype 3

- Which mode do you prefer: `1.7%`, `3.0%`, or `Both`?
- Does configurability help or confuse you?

## Prototype 4

- Does the visual scale improve understanding?
- Is it worth the screen space?

- Which version is easiest to understand?
- Which version fits best into your workflow?
- Which threshold strategy feels most appropriate?
- What would you change?
